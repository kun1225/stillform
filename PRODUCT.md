# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Design-conscious remote workers and makers who care about how their desk feels, not only what it does. They build calm work environments, notice material quality, and want focus tools that belong in the room instead of visually polluting it.

## Product Purpose
Stillform creates a physical focus object that combines an alarm clock and pomodoro timer into one refined desktop device. The website should present the product as a beautiful, credible tool for focused work and move interested visitors to join the waitlist.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, refined, disciplined. The voice is sparse, grounded, and tactile, with short sentences and precise sensory language. It should feel closer to a thoughtful industrial design book than to a startup campaign.

## Anti-references
Do not look like a generic SaaS landing page, a gadget crowdfunding page full of badges, or a loud productivity-hack brand. Avoid neon tech aesthetics, dashboard-style metrics, oversized gradient effects, and over-marketed claims.

## Design Principles
1. Treat the product like an object of industrial design, not a feature bundle.
2. Lead with atmosphere and material presence, then explain utility with restraint.
3. Reduce distraction in both product behavior and page composition.
4. Make every section feel deliberate enough to live beside a carefully arranged desk.
5. Keep conversion present but quiet, so the brand does not sound needy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, preserve clear heading structure, and keep motion subtle with reduced-motion fallbacks. Inputs and calls to action should remain keyboard accessible and readable across mobile and desktop layouts.
